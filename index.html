The Challenge

Four problems that make development activities fall short.

Most Learning & Development programmes address one or two of these challenges in isolation. We built a framework that tackles all four — in sequence, and as a system.

01

Causing indecision & disengagement

A relevance problem.

Learning & development isn't anchored to who someone actually is, what they need to progress or what their organisation needs from them to excel in their current and future roles.

02

Causing inaction & apathy

A motivation problem.

People can lack the motivation or engagement to sustain effort. Without addressing the will to succeed first, progress will stall and real change won't happen.

03

Causing disconnection & inefficiency

A scaling problem.

Individual growth rarely translates into collective and organisational performance improvement. Without deliberate design, the effect of development activity stays isolated.

04

Causing uncertainty & underinvestment

An impact problem.

Organisations often invest in development without knowing whether it worked, what it was worth, or how to meaningfully improve it. Measurement must address individual progress & happiness, team performance and organisational return on investment.

The Solution

A framework to tackle all four challenges — in sequence, and as a system.

Skill, Will, Connectivity and Measurement are four interdependent elements of a single diagnostic and development system. Each one addresses a specific problem. Together, they produce learning & development progress that sticks, scales and lasts.

Solving the relevance problem

Skill

Identify the skills that matter most — for this person, this role, this organisation.

Solving the relevance problem

Identify and develop the skills that matter most.

  • Analyse data to prioritise development needs.
  • Map to personal goals and organisational objectives.
  • Prioritise actions based on what's needed now and next.
  • Address the capacity to learn new skills, adapt and be flexible.

Solving the motivation problem

Will

Before any development begins, we understand how to make it stick.

Solving the motivation problem

Develop the mindset, energy and resilience to make change stick.

  • Assess individual and team motivation levels.
  • Understand each person's values, needs and goals.
  • Enhance motivation, mindset, resilience and energy.
  • Build reps, track progress and optimise the will to change.

Solving the scaling problem

Connectivity

Design development to scale — from individual to team to organisation.

Solving the scaling problem

Scale the effect through collaboration and shared purpose.

  • Align goals across individuals, teams and organisation.
  • Build habits through interaction, immersion and collaboration.
  • Build relational capability — empathy, influence and trust.
  • Create collective impact — the multiplier effect through human collaboration.

Solving the impact problem

Measurement

Predict ROI before you begin. Track and optimise as you go.

Solving the impact problem

Predict, track and optimise ROI throughout.

  • Set targets and predict ROI before the programme begins.
  • Track progress against targets at regular intervals.
  • Scenario plan and optimise the activity mix based on evidence.
  • Measure true bottom-line value generated.

The Tools

Data and analytics at every stage.

Our tools use data and analytics throughout — to diagnose where development is needed, personalise the approach, track progress, and optimise ROI as we go. And underpinning this is the safe and responsible use of AI to make it quicker, easier and more fulfilling for people to take meaningful action.

Tool 01

Being & Becoming

A personal development space designed to reinforce your authentic self. A running reminder of who you are, and a way to spot the parts of your life most worth your attention. It's somewhere to check in honestly, reflect and recognise what's already going well. From there, it helps surface small, realistic changes that genuinely lift your happiness and fulfilment — the kind you can make calmly, steadily, and on your own terms. And when you're confronted with a new challenge or want a fresh take on a situation, it enables you to plan and test what might work for you. It helps make development an enjoyable and sustainable pursuit, keeping skill, will and connectivity visible, and growth on track.

Individual reflection, progress checking and exploration

Tool 02

Learning & Development Planner

An organisational planning tool that benchmarks current talent performance, identifies priority skill gaps, sets measurable targets, and predicts the ROI before any programme begins. As the programme runs and progress is tracked, it measures the true bottom-line value generated. Every engagement starts with a defined investment case — and ends with evidence of the return.
